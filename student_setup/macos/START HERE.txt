FLIGHTLABFOAM FOR macOS

1. Double-click "Start FlightLabFoam.command".
2. Keep the launcher window open while you use FlightLabFoam in your browser.
3. Click Exit in the app, or close the launcher window, when you are finished.

The first launch needs internet access and may take a few minutes. Later
launches are faster. FlightLabFoam checks for updates automatically.

If macOS blocks the downloaded file, Control-click "Start FlightLabFoam.command",
choose Open, and then choose Open again. You normally need to do this only once.

If setup fails, take a screenshot of the launcher window and send it to your TA.
