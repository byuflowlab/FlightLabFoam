FLIGHTLABFOAM FOR LINUX

1. Open a terminal in this folder and run:  bash "Start FlightLabFoam.sh"
2. Keep the terminal open while you use FlightLabFoam in your browser.
3. Click Exit in the app, or press Control-C in the terminal, when you are finished.

The first launch needs internet access and may take a few minutes. Later
launches are faster. FlightLabFoam checks for updates automatically.

The launcher needs curl or wget, which nearly every distribution includes.

If setup fails, take a screenshot of the terminal and send it to your TA.
